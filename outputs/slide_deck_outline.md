# Slide Deck Outline

## 1. Executive Takeaway

The dataset shows a clear pattern: reliability incidents, especially around Detect visibility and alerting, become renewal and competitive risk when they touch compliance-heavy customers.

## 2. Dataset and Method

- 100 meetings processed.
- Call types inferred as Support, External Customer, and Internal.
- Themes assigned with a transparent keyword taxonomy.
- Sentiment uses supplied meeting-level scores plus transcript sentence sentiment as supporting evidence.

## 3. Sentiment by Call Type

Use `sentiment_by_call_type.svg`.

Message: Support calls are the lowest-sentiment segment, while external customer calls show where operational pain turns into commercial pressure.

## 4. Theme Landscape

Use `theme_counts.svg`.

Message: Product roadmap and compliance themes are common, but reliability and competitive pressure are the strongest risk signals.

## 5. Highest-Risk Accounts and Meetings

Use `risk_register.csv`.

Message: The top risks center on loss of threat visibility, failed proactive communication, and compliance exposure.

## 6. What Leadership Should Do

- Prioritize Detect reliability and alerting as revenue protection.
- Proactively message compliance customers after incidents.
- Track competitor mentions after outages.
- Review support escalations weekly with product and engineering.

## 7. Bonus Insight Ideas

- Renewal risk early warning.
- Product-module pain map.
- Action accountability by owner/function.
- Incident communication quality scoring.
